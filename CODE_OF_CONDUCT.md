# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participants should focus on the work, assume good faith, and give actionable technical feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.
